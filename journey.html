In the app: Candidates → Add. Ravi enters name, client, job, start date, W2, bill rates ($90 / OT $135), pay rates ($40 / OT $60), work state CA, recruiter Arjun, sales person Neha and business line Medical Solutions. On Per diem he enters her home city (Phoenix) and work city (Los Angeles). Because she travels over 50 miles, the app allows per diem up to the GSA rate, $171/day.
How her per diem works 1 · Is she allowed it? Yes, because she keeps her home in Phoenix and works in LA, more than 50 miles away. Someone who lives in LA gets no per diem. 2 · How much? The government (GSA) sets a daily rate for each city: hotel $107 + meals $64 = $171/day for LA (sample figures). Generis Tek can pay up to this amount without tax. 3 · Which days? Every day she is on assignment, weekends included: 7 × $171 = $1,197 a week. 4 · How is it paid? With her weekly pay through ADP, as a separate tax-free line (code PDM). No tax is taken from it. 5 · What else? The client doesn't see it as a separate line, because it comes out of the bill rate. It is left out of the Workers' Comp report. If she goes home for a week, that week has no per diem. 6 · Travel days? On the day she travels to or from the assignment, per diem depends on how many hours she also worked that day:

Quarterly & yearly

Manager commission: Ravi and Kiran

Ravi (delivery manager) and Kiran (sales manager) aren't paid per worker. They earn a bonus when the run rate of all their new hires in a quarter or year reaches a slab. Both use Kekin's 2026 plans. Ravi is paid in dollars and Kiran in rupees.

Run rate = the hourly profit Generis Tek makes on her, using the plan's cost formula.

What if something goes differently?

The hospital hires Maria permanentlyRavi records it on Direct hires (FTE) and Finance invoices the placement fee. For Neha, if this is a new perm client, Sample 1 Part 2 applies: ₹40,000 after the warranty ends and the fee is paid.
Maria leaves in week 4Arjun's placement never qualifies (under 6 weeks). Neha's account payout keeps waiting for a worker who reaches 6 weeks (to confirm with Kekin).
She works a 13-hour shiftCalifornia rule: 8 regular, 4 overtime and 1 double time. Billed and paid at those rates automatically.
The client short-paysFinance records the amount actually received. The line shows Short paid with the balance and stays on the overdue email until it is settled.
A recruiter logs inAccess control decides what they see. Arjun sees his own commission but not bill rates or margins, unless he is ticked for them.
The ShiftWise password expiresThe screenshot fails and Admin gets the alert email. Ravi can still approve hours, and the screenshot is retaken once the login is fixed.
