Sales commission, start to finish

How a sales person earns money under Kekin's Sample 1 plan. There are three separate ways to earn. Each one has its own trigger, its own amount and its own pay date. All amounts are fixed rupee payouts, not a percentage of profit.

Part 1

New MSP account payout

An MSP (managed service provider) program is a large client's staffing program run through a VMS portal, with many suppliers competing for jobs. A program is only worth what Generis Tek can realistically win, so the plan sizes it by fair share.

Fair share = (total program spend − payroll, LI, HI) ÷ number of suppliers

ClassFair sharePayout
A$10M and above₹1,30,000
B$5M – $10M₹1,00,000
C$2M – $5M₹70,000
D$1M – $2M₹40,000
NoneBelow $1M (assumed)₹0

Part 2

New permanent-placement client

Every new direct-hire client starts as Class D. As more people are hired for that client in the first 12 months after it goes live, it moves up a class. Each step up pays only the difference, so the total never exceeds ₹1,30,000 per client.

A hire counts only once its warranty period is over (the candidate stayed) and the client has paid Generis Tek's fee.

Part 3

Quarterly hire bonus

Every quarter, hires are counted separately for each of three business lines: WPH, Focus One and Medical Solutions. Each line pays its own bonus and the three are added together.

Questions for Kekin

  1. Who gets paid? Only the sales person who won the account, or is it split, e.g. with a Sales Director?
  2. What are WPH, Focus One and Medical Solutions? Divisions, client groups or teams? Every hire needs to be tagged with one.
  3. Quarterly tiers: is ₹20,000 for 2 hires the total, or on top of the ₹9,000? Is 5 hires ₹48,000 + ₹13,000 = ₹61,000?
  4. What counts as a "hire"? A contract start, a perm placement, or both, for Part 3?
  5. How long is the warranty period for perm placements?
  6. Fair share bands: is $3M a Class C? What happens below $1M?
  7. Fair share data: who supplies the program spend and supplier count, and is it entered once or updated?
  8. Rupees only? Are payouts fixed in INR regardless of the dollar rate?
  9. Leaving: if the sales person leaves, are pending step-ups still paid?
  10. Sample 2: which roles does it cover, and does it replace or add to Sample 1?
